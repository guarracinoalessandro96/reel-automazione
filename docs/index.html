Reel Alessandro

A personal publishing tool for my own short videos.

What it is

I am Alessandro Guarracino, an entrepreneur based in Naples, Italy. Every day I record short clips of my real life: driving to work, running my e-commerce business, the gym, time with my family. Reel Alessandro is the tool I built to prepare these clips (vertical 9:16 format, a short motivational caption, music) and to publish them to my own TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

Uno strumento personale per pubblicare i miei brevi video sul mio account TikTok.

How it works

1. RecordI film short clips of my day with my phone.
2. PrepareThe tool formats each clip to 9:16, adds a caption and prepares a preview.
3. Review and postI log in with TikTok, check the preview, edit the caption, choose the privacy level and settings, then press "Post to TikTok".

Nothing is posted without my explicit action on the publishing page.

Features

  • Login with TikTok (Login Kit) showing my avatar and nickname
  • Video preview and editable caption before posting
  • Privacy level chosen by me from the options of my account, with no default
  • Comment, Duet and Stitch settings, disabled when they are off in my TikTok settings
  • Commercial content disclosure (Your brand / Branded content) and Music Usage Confirmation
  • Posting status shown after each upload
